## New

- **Fill your board from your AI assistant.** Add GrepThink as a custom connector in Claude or ChatGPT, sign in, and paste your scrum doc or backlog. The assistant adds the sprints, stories and tasks. It shows you the plan first and adds nothing until you say yes. The board's Connect an AI assistant button has the address, and Settings → Connected apps lets you disconnect an app.
- **Bring a board you already run.** A team with sprints somewhere else can move in through the connector, finished work included. Tasks with an estimate count in the burnup chart on the days they were done. A notice on the board invites your team in, and finished work can come across until the team has added more than 3 stories by hand.
