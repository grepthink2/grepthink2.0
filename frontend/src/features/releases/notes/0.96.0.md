## New

- **TAs can open late windows.** An instructor can let a TA accept late TSR submissions for the class: a switch on the TA's card in TA Management, with a count of the late windows that TA has opened.
- **Late submissions from TA Review.** A TA with that permission sees each TSR assignment's deadline and late window under the assignment picker, and can set, move or remove the window from there. A window more than two weeks past the deadline gets a warning first.
- **Students hear about it.** When a late window opens or is extended, the students who have not submitted get a notification that opens the class's Assignments page.
- **Late marks for reviewers.** In the TSR review tables, a submission made after the deadline is marked Late, and one submitted on time but changed after the deadline is marked Edited late.

## Fixed

- **Documents pages fit.** The Documents tab's Start button sits inside its row, the tables keep their widths without a stray scrollbar, and the page uses its full width.
