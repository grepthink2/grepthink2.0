## New

- **Project documents on the board.** The scrum board has a Documents tab with your team's sprint reports, release summary, and test plan and report. Each one fills in what the board already knows (stories, hours, rates, the burnup, acceptance criteria), so you write only the rest.
- **Hand in when it's ready.** Every save is a revision you can compare with an earlier one. The Scrum Master or Product Owner finalizes a document, which freezes what came from the board and tells your TAs. Download any document as Markdown.
- **Acceptance criteria on stories.** A story has its own list of acceptance criteria, which the release summary and the test plan read.
- **Put stories in order.** Move a story up or down within its sprint or the backlog.

## Improved

- **The week is on Home.** The schedule card on Home shows the week from today, and the separate Schedule page now opens Home.
- **Hand-ins on TA Meetings.** Instructors and TAs see how many documents each team has handed in.
