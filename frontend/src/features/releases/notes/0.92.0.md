## New

- **Sprints come from the class.** Create Class and Class Settings set the sprint schedule: how many sprints, weekly, every two weeks or monthly, and the day the first one starts. Every team gets its sprints ready-made, their status follows the calendar, and the schedule must end by the last week of classes.
- **Teams create sprints from the board.** An empty board offers Create a sprint, and the sprint picker ends with New sprint. Team members only.
- **The roster has pages.** Show 10, 25, 50 or 100 students at a time. The search narrows the students only: the TA list always shows in full.

## Improved

- The burnup chart draws in points when every task in the sprint has them, and in hours otherwise.
- The project page shows the project's icon beside its name.
- What's new has its own tab on the landing page, and the notes read better in the profile menu.

## Fixed

- Saving a task on a story in the backlog now reaches the server. The editor used to close as if it had.
