## New

- **What's new.** Notes like these now tell you what changed in each release: open them from your profile menu, or from the landing page.
- **TAs have their own list on the roster.** They appear apart from the students, without a course status, and the course filters now cover students only.

## Improved

- **A simpler Home for students and TAs.** Today's Schedule comes first, then your team and your requests. Deadlines are off Home for now: find them on the Assignments page.
- **Times follow your school.** The scrum board's days and the campus times shown beside your own follow your school's time zone instead of always Pacific time.
- The app opens faster: each page loads its own code the first time you visit it.
- A team meeting in progress shows a blue Live badge on the project page.

## Fixed

- The course filters on My Classes look right again.
- When Settings, Create Class or Join Class fails to load, you now get a Reload button instead of a blank page.
