# Handoff: Final-review slot sign-up (C2) — the board, Your team's review, posting and the instructor's tools

**Brief:** `uploads/2026-10-20-final-review-signup/README.md` (+ `PROMPT.md`, `sample-data.json`; PR #258, branch `docs/c2-final-review-signup-brief`) · **Decisions, §12 answers, kit additions, status keys, the §10 file list and port questions:** `NOTES.md` (this folder) · **Builds on:** `design_handoff_ui_alignment/` (the kit), `design_handoff_schedule/` (time-zone rule, `final_review` kind, `ScheduleDayHeader`), `design_handoff_team_meetings/` (form pattern, inline confirm, project-page card).

## Overview
One page and one address for every role — **`/app/final-reviews`** for the class selected in the sidebar — and the header of the scoring workspace at `/app/final-reviews/:projectId` (staff). The page is: the **summary bar** (sign-ups state for everyone, counts, the TA's review count, the shared Zoom room for staff, the instructor's **Post slots** and **Open / Close sign-ups**), **Your team's review** for students, **the board** (every slot by day in the viewer's zone, each with the viewer's actions), and **Teams without a slot** for staff. Product Owners book from the board, TAs sign up from it, the instructor runs everything from it. Students also get a **Final review** card on their project page. Everything is composed from the kit; the only kit addition is seven Badge status keys.

## About the design files
Design references in HTML/JSX — intended look and behaviour, not production code to copy. Recreate in grepthink2.0 (React 19 + TS + SCSS + Vite + Supabase) with its patterns: BEM SCSS, `--gt-*` custom properties, lucide-react (`ClipboardCheck`, `CalendarPlus`, `Video`, `Globe`, `Trash2`, `Ellipsis`, `ArrowLeft`, `ChevronRight`, `Plus`, `X`, `UserPlus`, `ArrowRightLeft`, `CalendarX`, `CalendarClock`, `Lock`, `Users`, `Pencil` — `FinalReviewsIcon` stands in for them). `components/finalreviews/*.jsx` are close to production shape (typed props in each `.d.ts`, the pattern and keyboard model in each `.prompt.md`); `FinalReviewsModel.jsx` is pure and ports as `features/finalReviews/finalReviewsModel.ts`. Styles are `components/finalreviews/finalreviews.css` — `.gt-fr-*` blocks, **no raw hex**, no kit block re-declared (kit components are adjusted only from `.gt-fr-*` classes).

## Interactions & keyboard
- Tab order on the board: day links → filter (one tab stop; arrows move and select) → per row: its buttons, then the Menu trigger. Menus: Popover + Menu (↑ ↓ Home End, Enter, Esc returns focus to the ⋯). Every row button's name carries the slot or team and starts with its visible label ("Book Mon, Dec 7 · 11:00 AM", "Sign up to review Redwood Labs", "More actions for Trailhead").
- Day links are in-page anchors to `#gt-fr-day-YYYY-MM-DD` (sections carry `scroll-margin-top`). "Past days (n)" is a disclosure (`aria-expanded` / `aria-controls`).
- **Book** acts at once (no confirm) with a busy "Booking…" button and a polite toast. **Move here**, **Release**, **Step down**, **Close sign-ups** and the day delete confirm (ConfirmDialog: focus on Cancel, Esc cancels, busy blocks Esc and the scrim). The instructor's dialogs focus the Select; Enter submits; a refusal shows inside and keeps the choice.
- Delete a free slot: immediate, toast "Deleted the Mon, Dec 7 · 2:00 PM slot." with **Undo** (re-posts that time).
- Refusals: the board refreshes (frame held, `aria-busy`) and a warning `Alert` above it says what happened, dismissible. Network failures: an error toast (row actions) or inside the dialog. Successes: polite toasts (`ToastStack`).
- Join Zoom appears from 10 minutes before the start until the end (schedule layer's window) for the people in the review — the team, its Review TA, the instructor; never for the Home TA. Live from the start.
- Reduced motion: the past chevron and the refetch fade lose their transitions; nothing else moves.

## Data contract
As the brief §8 (`ApiReviewSlots`, `ApiReviewSlot`, `ApiFinalReviewSchedule`, class TAs), with **`my_team.product_owner`** added by the C2 implementation (one Product Owner or null). Typed in `components/finalreviews/FinalReviewsModel.d.ts`. Requests and refusals as §7 — `errorCopy(action, { status, detail })` turns each into the board's sentence. **Changed in the C2 implementation:** a TA's sign-up carries the slot the TA saw — `POST /api/tas/projects/{project_id}/review-tas` `{ user_id?, slot_id }` — and the server answers **409** (detail "This team moved to another slot.") when the team holds a different slot than `slot_id`; the board refreshes and shows "Your sign-up for Redwood Labs didn’t go through: the team moved to Tue, Dec 8 · 10:00 AM after you loaded the board. Sign up again at the new time?" with **Sign up for Tue, Dec 8 · 10:00 AM** (which sends the new `slot_id`). The instructor's appoint carries no `slot_id` (any time, slot or not). `my_team.product_owner` names the **first** Product Owner when a team has several. Undo of a deleted slot re-posts the same start. Pre-slots classes assume 60-minute reviews. Posting sends `{ starts_at: ["2026-12-07T09:00-08:00", …] }` (campus offset, no seconds, no repeats). Classes from before slots: staff see `final_review_at` from the staff read as read-only rows (`historyRows`); students see the no-slots state.
