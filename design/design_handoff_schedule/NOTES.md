# NOTES — Schedule, 2026-10-17

Decisions that go beyond or depart from the brief, with reasons, plus the open questions the port has to close.

## Decisions

1. **Titles are the kind for recurring structure; the kind eyebrow only appears when the title doesn't say it.** "TA meeting", "Lecture", "Office hours", "Final review" read naturally as titles and keep the team / course in the context line, where the first token is set in medium weight so a TA scanning three teams in an afternoon still sees "Team 1 · Team 4 · Team 6" at a glance. One-off meetings and deadlines carry custom titles, so they get a small-caps **MEETING** / **DEADLINE** eyebrow in the kind color. Icon + label therefore never depend on color (the brief's rule); the tile tint is the third cue, not the first.
2. **Kind palette from existing tokens.** Green = the team's own weekly meeting (the product's primary action color), purple = final review (the categorical slot 3 already used for stories; the Final Reviews nav icon is `ClipboardCheck`, reused here), accent blue = one-off meetings (info), gray = lectures (background structure), white-with-dashed-outline = office hours (the dashed treatment already means "optional / preview" in this system), amber = deadlines (the same tint the search layer gives assignments). Gold and red are untouched. All pairs are ≥ 4.5:1 (green-700 on green-50 6.3:1, purple on white 7:1, info-text on info-soft, gray-700 on gray-100 8.6:1, warning-text on white 6.4:1).
3. **Time zones: label once, campus time on hover.** Times always render in the viewer's zone (the codebase's final-review formatters already do), the mismatch is said once per list, and each `<time>` carries the campus time in its `title`. Inline "3:00 PM PT · 1:00 AM your time" on every row was rejected for the compact column (≈300px) — it doubles every time string and pushes the one the viewer acts on into the noise. If an inline form is wanted later, show it only on the live/next item.
4. **Day grouping follows the viewer's zone too.** A Monday 1:20 PM PT lecture lists under Tuesday for a viewer in IST — that is the day they'd have to be awake for it. The alternative (group by campus day, show viewer times) produces days that contain tomorrow's small hours.
5. **Join Zoom is a link, styled as the primary button only inside the window.** 10 minutes before → end (`JOIN_WINDOW_MS`). Before the window the agenda offers a quiet outlined "Zoom" so the link is reachable; the compact card shows nothing until the window opens (calm by default, prominent when it matters). After the end there is no link.
6. **The Now line sits between rows, not on a time grid.** The list is an agenda, so "now" is a position in the sequence: after everything that has ended, before the first item still to come; trailing when the day is over; absent when the day has only all-day items. The live row spans it and gets the Live pill.
7. **Sprint edges are not `<li>`s.** `ScheduleDivider` renders between the day header and the day's `<ol>`, so screen readers don't count "Sprint 3 starts" as an event and it can never receive a time, an action or a card.
8. **Deadlines are a one-line marker.** No tile fill, an amber flag, 13/500 — visibly lighter than any meeting, as the brief asks, and still in the day's order (11:59 PM lands last).
9. **Agenda shows the days that have something, plus today.** Seven headers with four "Nothing scheduled" lines padded the week; `showEmptyDays` renders all seven if the data layer prefers it.
10. **Kind chips reuse the search layer's chip spec** (`.gt-scope` values) under their own class name, because `features/search` hasn't started yet; merge the SCSS when both exist.
11. **Past rows dim to 0.55 but their pills and actions don't.** A student's attendance and a TA's "Mark attendance" are the reason to look at a past row.
12. **"Overlaps" is a pill, not a bracket or a color.** Each overlapping item names its partners in the pill's `title` and in visually-hidden text; `findOverlaps` ignores deadlines, cancelled and TBD items.
13. **Compact card chrome belongs to the page.** The card header ("Today's Schedule" + the date, 12px tertiary) is the existing `.student-home__card-header`; `ScheduleList` owns the body and the footer (zone note, "+n more today", "Add to your calendar", "See week →"), so the TA and Instructor homes can reuse it unchanged.
14. **Demo clock is pinned.** The kit renders at Tue Oct 20, 2026 3:12 PM PT (live), 9:00 AM, 8:30 PM and Sat Oct 24 (empty); production uses `useNow()` with no `now` prop.

## Open questions (for Claude Code)

- **Schedule endpoint.** Nothing server-side aggregates these. Proposed `GET /api/schedule?from=&to=` returning `ScheduleItemData[]` for the caller's lens (RLS: own classes/teams), built from `ta_meetings` (expand `meeting_day` + `meeting_time` + `meeting_duration_minutes` × `meetings_per_week` into dated occurrences for the range, attach `viewer_status` / tallies), `final_reviews` (`final_review_at`, `home_ta`, `review_ta`, `review_zoom_url`), `assignments.due_at`, `scrum_sprints.starts_at/ends_at` (per project). Return `status: 'tbd'` for a TA meeting with no time yet.
- **Lectures, office hours and one-off meetings have no tables.** Proposed `class_events` (`class_id`, `kind: lecture | office_hours | meeting`, `title`, `starts_at`, `ends_at`, `location`, `zoom_url`, `host_user_id`, `scope`, `project_id?`, `note`, `rrule?`, `cancelled_at`, `cancel_reason`). Recurrence can start as a weekly rule + exception rows; expansion happens server-side so the client only ever sees instances.
- **Sprint edges for staff.** Sprints are per project, so an instructor with 12 teams would get 12 rules a week. Options: show none for staff, show only the selected team's, or roll up ("Sprint 3 starts · 8 teams"). The component renders whatever is sent; the sample uses "Sprint 2 · Team 1" labels for the TA.
- **Instructor's TA meetings.** Individually they can be 10+ rows a day. Either send them only when a team filter is active, or add a roll-up row kind later ("6 TA meetings · 3:00 – 6:00 PM → TA Meetings"). Designed as individual rows.
- **"Add to your calendar".** Needs a per-user tokenized ICS feed (`/api/schedule/calendar.ics?token=`) with `VTIMEZONE` for `America/Los_Angeles`; instances, not rules, keep clients consistent with the app.
- **Set time** (staff, Time TBD) should open the existing `AddZoomModal` (day + time + Zoom URL); **Mark attendance** should open the inline check-in roster from `TeamMeetingCard` or route to `/app/ta-meetings` with the team expanded. The design only emits the callbacks.
- **Route.** `/app/schedule` is new; add it to `sidebar.ts` for all three roles (icon `CalendarDays`) and to `routePermissions.ts`. "See week" from the card and the `?day=` deep link (open the agenda scrolled to a day) are the two entry points.
- **Which "today" during preview mode.** "View class as student" should pass the previewed lens's items; the component doesn't care.
- **Live tick.** `useNow` re-renders every 30s; if the list is large, memoize rows by `id + status + joinable` or lift the tick to the page.
- **Zone preference.** The design reads the browser zone. If a profile-level zone setting exists later, pass it as `zone`; the campus zone should come from the class (`schoolZone`), not a constant, once classes outside PT exist (the dateUtils tests already model an Istanbul school).
- **Attendance vocabulary.** Reuses `STATUS_LABEL` (Present / Late / Absent / Not marked) from `taTypes.ts`; keep the pill tones in sync if those labels change.
- **Empty-state copy** ("Class events and deadlines will appear here.") is the string the current card already promises; keep it verbatim when the real data lands.
