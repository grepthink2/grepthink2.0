# NOTES — Team meetings, 2026-10-18

Decisions that go beyond or depart from the brief, with reasons, plus the open questions the port has to close.

## Decisions

1. **The card hides for members and shows a quiet line for the Product Owner.** A permanently empty "Team meetings" card on every member's project page is noise, and the Today's Schedule card already promises "class events and deadlines". The one person who can fix the emptiness is the PO, so only they see "No team meetings yet. Add one" (→ the Meetings tab). `showTeamMeetingsCard()` encodes the rule; the card appears for everyone the moment the first meeting exists.
2. **Meetings save on their own; the modal footer shows "Done" on that tab.** Details and Team are a form that "Save Changes" commits as a batch. Meetings are a list of records, each with its own Add / Save, so a trailing "Save Changes" would imply they aren't saved yet. Delete Project stays where it is. If one footer for all tabs is preferred, keep Cancel / Save Changes and make Save a no-op for meetings — but the hint under the tab should then say meetings save immediately.
3. **Inline form, not a second modal.** A modal over a modal costs the user their place in the list and the system a second focus trap; the tab body is already a 580px scrolling sheet. The form takes the row's place (edit) or the top of the list (new), on the muted surface the schedule layer already uses for an expanded row. One editor at a time: Add and the other rows' Edit / Delete are disabled while one is open, so there is never an unsaved draft hiding below the fold.
4. **Native `date` and `time` inputs.** They are what the Final Reviews time field uses (`datetime-local`), they work inside a scrolling modal body (a popover calendar would clip or extend the scroll), and they give a 15-minute step for free. The app's `DatePickerField` can replace the date input later without touching the model.
5. **Duration is a select, not a free number.** 15-minute steps to 2 h, then 2 h 30 / 3 h / 4 h. Typing "50" and being corrected is worse than picking "45 min". If longer meetings are needed, extend `TEAM_MEETING_DURATIONS`.
6. **Room and Zoom can both be set.** Hybrid meetings exist. The row shows the room with a pin and "Zoom" as a link; the schedule row shows the room in the context line and the Zoom as the Join action.
7. **No-past-start applies to creating only.** Editing a meeting that already happened (fixing a note, a room) must stay possible, and moving an upcoming meeting is an edit too; the rule exists to stop accidental back-dated entries. A meeting in progress counts as upcoming and wears the Live pill.
8. **Time zones follow the schedule handoff.** Typed and shown on the viewer's clock; the mismatch is said once per list ("Times in GMT+5:30 · campus is PT"), each time's `title` carries the campus time, and the form's time field shows "2:30 AM PT · Tue on campus" so a PO abroad never books the team at 3 AM by mistake.
9. **Delete confirm lands focus on Cancel.** The sentence names the meeting and the consequence; Enter on the confirm's Delete is the second, deliberate press. Esc cancels. The confirm never opens a dialog.
10. **Friendly errors, not API messages.** "Couldn't save this meeting. Your changes are still here — try again." / "Couldn't delete. Try again." / "Couldn't load team meetings." The response text goes to the console; a status-specific line (e.g., 403 "Only the Product Owner can change meetings") can be added by the port.
11. **Long titles wrap.** Titles are capped at 80 characters by the field, so a row can afford two or three lines; truncating the one line that identifies the meeting would hide the difference between "Final review rehearsal — slides" and "Final review rehearsal — demo". Notes truncate to one line instead (full text in `title`).
12. **Demo clock is pinned** at Tue Oct 20, 2026 3:12 PM PT so "Sprint 3 planning" (3:00 – 4:00) is live; production uses `useNow()`.

## Open questions (for Claude Code)

- **Who is "the Product Owner"?** The brief says only the team's Product Owner (Scrum `project_role = 'product owner'`) can add, edit or delete, and nobody else sees the tab. Today `EditProjectModal` opens for `canManageProject` (project owner / admins / staff). Decide: (a) strictly the PO; (b) PO + the project owner as a fallback when no PO is assigned yet (new teams have none); (c) also class staff. The design gates everything on one boolean (`canManage` / `viewerIsProductOwner`), so any answer is a one-line change.
- **Table.** `team_meetings` (per project) or the schedule handoff's `class_events` with `kind = 'meeting'`, `scope = 'team'`, `project_id`. One table keeps `GET /api/schedule` and the ICS feed simple; a dedicated table keeps RLS obvious. The data shape in the README is the same either way.
- **Visibility in the schedule.** Team members see their team's meetings as kind `meeting`. Should the home TA see them too (they're invited to a design review)? The schedule handoff's TA lens lists "staff meetings"; a `visibleToStaff` flag or the TA's assignment to the team could decide.
- **Calendar feed.** Add these as `VEVENT`s to the per-user ICS feed proposed in `design_handoff_schedule/NOTES.md`; `updatedAt` → `SEQUENCE`/`LAST-MODIFIED` so edits propagate, deletes → `STATUS:CANCELLED` or removal.
- **Notifications.** Nothing here notifies the team. A toast for the PO ("Meeting added") is cheap; a message in the team thread or a notification for members when a meeting is created / moved / deleted is a product decision.
- **Conflicts.** The schedule layer's `findOverlaps()` can warn the PO when a new meeting overlaps the team's TA meeting or a lecture; the form would need the team's schedule for the chosen day. Designed without it.
- **Deleting a past meeting.** Allowed in the design (the PO owns the list). If attendance or notes ever hang off meetings, switch past rows to Edit only.
- **Esc in the modal.** `EditProjectModal` closes on a document-level Escape. The form and the confirm call `stopPropagation` on Esc so the first press only cancels them; the port must keep that order (or move the modal's listener to the sheet).
- **Recurring team meetings** are out of scope (weekly meetings are the TA's). If a team wants a standing sync, the `rrule` column proposed for `class_events` would cover it; the form would gain a "Repeats" select.
