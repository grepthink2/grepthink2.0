"How this is counted" popover for a chart-card header. 320px, sentence case, opens from an ⓘ icon button.

```jsx
<DefinitionPopover body="Messages sent in team-member channels of the teams in view, plus direct messages between two people of the school, in the selected range." />
```

Production composes the `Popover` primitive (`components/feedback/`); this reference reuses its `.gt-popover` classes directly.
