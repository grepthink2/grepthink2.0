Initials avatar from a name or email (deterministic color), plus `AvatarGroup` with "+n" overflow.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Ashton Liu" />
<Avatar name="jdoe@ucsc.edu" size="sm" />
<AvatarGroup names={['Ashton Liu','Tony W','Landon N','Joshua C','Priya R']} max={4} />
```

`square` for project avatars; `src` for photo avatars.
