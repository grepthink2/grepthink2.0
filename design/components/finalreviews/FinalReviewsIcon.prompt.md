Decorative lucide-shaped glyph for the final-review family (production imports lucide-react: ClipboardCheck, CalendarPlus, Video, Globe, Trash2, Ellipsis, ArrowLeft, ChevronRight, Plus, X, UserPlus, ArrowRightLeft, CalendarX, CalendarClock, Lock, Users, Pencil).

```jsx
<FinalReviewsIcon name="clipboard-check" size={20} />
```
Always `aria-hidden`; the control it sits in carries the name.
