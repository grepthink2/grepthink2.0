Uppercase pill label above a marketing headline, optionally with a NEW / SOON badge. In reading order it comes right before the heading ("New · Scrum board").

```jsx
<Eyebrow>For instructors and student teams</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow badge="NEW">Scrum board</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow badge="SOON" tone="amber">Project assistant</Eyebrow>
<MarketingBadge tone="soon">SOON</MarketingBadge>
```

Contrast: eyebrow text `#016547` on the green tint (6.3:1); NEW badge white on `#018156` (4.9:1); SOON badge white on `#8A5200` (6.4:1), never `#B26A00`.
