Clickable hero announcement: NEW badge · text · arrow, one link. Replaces the hero eyebrow while a launch is being announced (the audience line moves into the subtitle); one config flag turns it off.

```jsx
<AnnouncementPill href="#scrum-board"
  ariaLabel="New: scrum boards and team channels. Jump to the scrum board section">
  Scrum boards and team channels
</AnnouncementPill>
```

White pill, 1px green ring + soft green glow; arrow nudges 3px on hover; 2px blue focus ring.
