Icon-only button for toolbars, close affordances and overflow menus. Requires `ariaLabel`.

```jsx
<IconButton ariaLabel="Close" onClick={close}><X size={18} /></IconButton>
<IconButton ariaLabel="More" variant="ghost"><MoreVertical size={18} /></IconButton>
```

Variants: `ghost` · `solid` · `danger`. Sizes: `sm` · `md` · `lg`.
